Help & Cybercrime Resources

ScamAlert is a community-driven platform for marking fraudulent websites in India. Use it to check a suspicious link before you pay, report fraud you have seen, and help others verify. For money already lost, always call 1930 first, then file on cybercrime.gov.in.

What is ScamAlert?

Anyone can submit a fraud report with a URL, title, description, category, and evidence (screenshots, receipts). The community then verifies it with votes, comments, and discussions. Moderators review the queue and archive abuse.

Every website gets a two-axis risk score that separates technically malicious from community-reported fraud:

  • Technical risk — automated signals (lookalike domain, suspicious TLD, missing HTTPS, young WHOIS).
  • Fraud risk — community signals (report count, confirm votes, severity of category).

Final = (Community × 0.40) + (Automated × 0.40) + (Category Severity × 0.20). Each axis is 0–100.

Check a website before you trust it

  1. Go to Search and paste the full URL, domain, or keywords.
  2. Open the report at Reports and read the risk pill, score bars, status, evidence files, vote counts, and comment thread.
  3. Still unsure? Open the linked Discussion for that website, or start one, and ask the community.
  4. Check WHOIS age, HTTPS, and spelling tricks (extra dashes, homoglyphs, free-TLD lookalikes) on the safety checklist below.

Submit a good report

  1. Submit a report — you must be signed in. Guests cannot file reports or votes, but can comment (shown as Anonymous).
  2. Pick the exact category from the dropdown.
  3. Write a clear title (“Fake UPI cashback site impersonating…”) and describe what happened, when, how much money, and which URL asked for payment or OTP.
  4. Tick “Submit anonymously” if you do not want your name shown — moderators still see the record.
  5. Attach evidence, then download your complaint draft from the report page for the police / NCRP filing.

Evidence tips

  • Screenshots of the site, payment page, UPI handle, chat history, SMS, and bank/UPI transaction IDs.
  • Files up to 10 MB each, from an 11-type allowlist (images, PDF, text).
  • Uploaded files are viewable at their /uploads/… links with image thumbnails on the report page.
  • Blur Aadhaar, full card numbers, and passwords before uploading.

Reading risk scores, levels, and stages

Risk pills: critical high medium low safe other. Higher means riskier. Treat critical / high as do-not-pay.

Stages: reported → under review → verified / disputed → archived. History on each report page shows every transition.

Community verification & reputation

  • Reports and votes require login. You can comment anonymously.
  • Voting additionally requires reputation ≥ 60 — new accounts can report and comment first, then earn voting rights.
  • Tiers: newcomer new trusted expert moderator. Good reports and accurate votes raise reputation.
  • See who is helping on the Reputation Leaderboard.

Appeals

If your site was flagged by mistake, file an appeal from the report or website page. A moderator reviews evidence and marks it upheld, overturned, or rejected. Track progress on the appeal page. Provide ownership proof (domain control, business registration, official contact).

IT Act Sections

Section 66C Identity Theft. Section 66D — Cheating by Personation. Section 43 Damage to Computer System. Mention the relevant section and attach your ScamAlert complaint draft plus the NCRP acknowledgement number when visiting the police station.

How to file a complaint

  1. Save evidence (screenshots, URLs, transaction IDs).
  2. Call 1930 for urgent financial fraud.
  3. File on cybercrime.gov.in (NCRP).
  4. Download your ScamAlert complaint draft from the report page (DOCX via the complaint button).
  5. Visit your nearest police station with the acknowledgement number.
  6. Report phishing to CERT-In at incident@cert-in.org.in.

Safety tips for UPI, OTP, and KYC fraud

  • Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or screen-share on a “support” call. Banks never ask for them.
  • Verify UPI IDs character-by-character; scammers swap one letter or add “support”, “refund”, “kyc”.
  • Beware “pay ₹1 for KYC / cashback / job” links — real KYC never happens over random links.
  • Check the domain: misspellings, extra hyphens, free subdomains, and newly registered sites are red flags.
  • Do not install remote-desktop or “support” APKs from links. Download apps only from official stores.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
For reports and votes, yes. For comments, no guests post as Anonymous.

Why can’t I vote?
Voting needs reputation ≥ 60. Submit accurate reports and helpful comments to earn it.

Is my report anonymous?
Only if you tick “Submit anonymously”. Your account still owns the record for moderation and appeals.

Where is my complaint letter?
On the report page, the complaint button downloads a DOCX draft for NCRP / police use.

Why was my submit rejected?
Common causes: wrong category, hit the hourly rate limit, unsupported evidence file type, or duplicate URL already reported search first.

How does search work?
It merges full-text title/description hits with domain and keyword matches and dedupes by report, so searching a bare domain still finds the report.
